There are still 60 days left of 2026 and that's plenty of time to get a jump start on your 2027 fitness goals.
<!--more-->
<br>
<br>
It's November and if you haven't reached your fitness goals in 2026, it's still not too late. This year may have felt like it went by too fast, but we shouldn't let the fact that 83.29% of it is over to hold us back. 

If your goal is to lose weight, don't try to lose weight without strength training. You don't want to lose muscle mass if you're just focused on bringing the number on the scale down. 

I've created this weekly PDF to help you target and complete the number of sets per muscle group per week. Regardless of how many times you strength train per week, this weekly chart will help you check, at a glance, if you've trained each muscle group. Aim for 10 sets per week per muscle group for beginners and 10-15 sets per muscle group for intermediate strength training.
